About

I work at the intersection of epidemiology, data engineering, and public-interest design.

For more than a decade, I've used the methods of disease epidemiology — causal inference, surveillance, geospatial analysis — to study gun violence and urban safety. My research has appeared in The Lancet, BMJ, JAMA Surgery, JAMA Psychiatry, and over two dozen other peer-reviewed journals, and has been cited more than 600 times.

At Vital City, I direct data and research strategy, build interactive tools used by journalists, policymakers, and the public, and write data-driven analysis on what's happening in New York City. I designed and built every public data tool you'll see below.
